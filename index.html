About Me

  • PhD candidate (until Dec 2026)
  • Paris, France
École Normale Supérieure Université Paris Cité be-ys Research

My interests in AI are pretty broad, but they mostly come back to perception and representation: computer vision, representation learning, and JEPA-style world models. Reinforcement learning and vision-language models are never far behind. My PhD focuses on one small part of all this: teaching models to read documents, where the layout, tables and figures matter as much as the text itself. Away from research, I just like to build things. I'm comfortable in several languages (Python and C++ among them) and enjoy computing at every level, right down to low-level and embedded work. And I'm just as comfortable on the theory side, happy to take a problem all the way down to its mathematical foundations.
