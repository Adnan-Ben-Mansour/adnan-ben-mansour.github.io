Dense Per-Step Rewards for Reinforcement-Learned Reading Order in Scene Text Recognition

A. Ben Mansour, A. Karine, D. Naccache

ECCV 2026 Workshop

Paper →

We use RL with a dense reward to train a CTC-based encoder-only model to do scene text recognition, we use the plackett-luce distribution and ctc-loss to derive this reward, learning an explicit, inspectable reading order over the image patches.

Interpret, Prune and Distill Donut: Towards Lightweight VLMs for VQA on Documents

A. Ben Mansour, A. Karine, D. Naccache

ICDAR 2025 Workshop

Paper →

Uses mechanistic interpretability to guide pruning and distillation of the Donut VLM, yielding Donut-MINT, a lighter variant that keeps DocVQA performance at a fraction of the compute and memory.

Visualisation des interactions multimodales dans les modèles vision-langages

A. Ben Mansour, A. Karine

ORASIS 2025 Oral

Paper →

Presents visualisation methods that expose how multiple modalies interact in vision-language models, making modality collapse easier to read and diagnose.

Tackling Computational Heterogeneity in Federated Learning: A Few Theoretical Insights

A. Ben Mansour, G. Carenini, A. Duplessis

ARXIV 2023 Preprint

Paper →

Introduces an aggregation framework for federated learning that formalises and handles computational heterogeneity across clients with different data and local update budgets.

Federated learning aggregation: New robust algorithms with guarantees

A. Ben Mansour, G. Carenini, A. Duplessis, D. Naccache

ICMLA 2022 Poster

Paper →

Proposes robust federated learning aggregation rules that weight clients by the value of their losses, with full convergence proofs validated on IID and non-IID benchmarks.

FedControl: When Control Theory Meets Federated Learning

A. Ben Mansour, G. Carenini, A. Duplessis, D. Naccache

ICML 2022 Workshop

Paper →

Reframes federated aggregation through control theory. Inspired by PID controllers, we adapt the server step from each client's local performance trajectory rather than averaging coordinates blindly.
